Create a payment escrow

The wallet that deposits test ETH becomes the buyer. The seller may view the escrow but cannot withdraw it directly.

Your wallet signs transactions. This website never requests or stores private keys.

How the contract works

  1. Funded: The buyer deposits ETH into the contract.
  2. Released: The buyer sends the escrowed ETH to the seller.
  3. Refunded: After the deadline, the buyer may retrieve unreleased funds.
Educational limitation: No independent dispute resolution. The buyer controls release, and may refund after the deadline even if work has been performed.

Always use Sepolia test ETH, never real ETH.
